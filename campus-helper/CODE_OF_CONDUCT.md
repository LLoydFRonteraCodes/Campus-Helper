# Code of Conduct

We want campus-helper to be a friendly place to learn, especially for beginners.

**Please:**
- Be respectful and patient with everyone.
- Welcome questions, and give feedback kindly.
- Accept feedback on your own work gracefully.

**Please don't:**
- Insult, harass, or discriminate against anyone.
- Spam, or copy someone else's work and present it as your own.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, PRs, or issues that break these rules, and may
ask people to leave the project. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through
their GitHub profile.
